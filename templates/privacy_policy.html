🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, place an order, or contact us, we collect basic details such as your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address so we can process your orders.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, manage your cart and wishlist, send order updates, and improve your shopping experience with Pratham Ice Cream. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We use session cookies to keep you logged in and to remember the items in your cart while you browse the site.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Your password is stored securely and is never visible to our staff.

5. Your Rights

You can update your account details or request removal of your data at any time by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Us
